Privacy policy: Alien Shenanigans MCP

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Alien Shenanigans MCP is a private tool used only by the owner of the Alien Shenanigans YouTube channel to manage that channel (uploading, scheduling and editing videos, posting comments and reading channel analytics). It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: only the owner's own YouTube channel data, through Google's YouTube APIs, after the owner signs in.

How data is used: only to carry out actions the owner asks for. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer.

Sharing: no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
