Privacy policy: Alien Shenanigans tools

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy covers two private tools used only by the owner of the Alien Shenanigans channels. Neither is offered to the public.

  • Alien Shenanigans MCP: manages the owner's YouTube channel (uploading, scheduling and editing videos, posting comments and reading channel analytics).
  • Creator HQ: a password-protected dashboard that shows the owner's own performance statistics from YouTube, Google Search Console, Twitch and TikTok.

What they access

Only the owner's own accounts, after the owner signs in:

  • YouTube and Google Search Console (Google APIs): channel and video statistics, analytics, and search performance for this website.
  • Twitch: public channel information such as follower count, past broadcasts and top games.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Display API, scopes user.info.basic, user.info.stats and video.list): the owner's profile name, follower, following and like counts, and the owner's public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show the owner how their own content performs and to carry out actions the owner asks for. Creator HQ stores its data encrypted (AES-256) and it can only be read with the owner's password. Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets or on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about any other person is collected.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and Twitch under Settings → Connections.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
